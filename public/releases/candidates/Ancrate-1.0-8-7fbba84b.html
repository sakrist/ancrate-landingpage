Ancrate 1.0 candidate

This build is available for upload testing. Final distribution verification is still in progress.

Bring Apple Notes tasks together and keep formatting close to your writing.

  • Collect checklists across notes, including lists for the past month and past three months. Choose source folders or exclude individual notes.
  • Type / in Apple Notes for native formatting commands. Open collected tasks with /tasks, /month and /quarter.
  • Enable Markdown typing shortcuts for headings, checklists, monostyled text, quotes and lists.
  • Enable a compact formatting bar above selected text for styles, text size, emphasis, links, alignment and checklists.
  • Keep tasks nearby with the menu-bar panel and desktop widgets.
  • Connect a local AI client through four read-only MCP tools.
  • Check for signed app updates from Settings or the Ancrate menu. Automatic checking is optional and starts off.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Select the Notes folder during setup; optional Notes integration uses Accessibility permission.
